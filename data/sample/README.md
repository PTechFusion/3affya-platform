# Sample data

Synthetic, for the prototype only. Never put a real patient document or a real NPI record here; real registry data arrives through `scripts/` in task 1.5 and real patient data never leaves production.

- `doctors.json`: 20 doctors, 8 verified, 4 pending, 8 unclaimed. Field names match the planned schema (task 1.4).
- `specialties.json`: 24 of the 104 specialty groups, bilingual, with sample counts. The full list comes from the content document in task 1.5.
- `bookings.json`: three bookings in the three money states the patient portal shows. Amounts in minor units; the three lines sum to the total.
- `body-systems.json`: the body systems that group the specialty directory's full list (screen 4), bilingual, each listing specialty slugs. Editorial draft for Dr Ayman; the 104-group mapping comes with task 1.5.
- `invites.json`: three claim invites for screen 9 (`/claim/[token]`): `demo-valid` for `doc_013` (unclaimed), `demo-expired` for `doc_014`, `demo-used` for `doc_010` (already claimed, so that profile is pending). Any other token is forged. Real invites store only a hash of the token and expire 14 days after sending (task 2.3); the valid demo expires far ahead so the prototype link keeps working. Emails use the reserved `example.com` domain.
- `profiles.json`: one row per doctor with what the profile page (screens 6 and 7) shows beyond the card: group practice and the registry source and date for unclaimed and pending rows; the named board, licence state and the next open times (UTC) for verified rows. `slotsUtc[0]` equals the doctor's `nextAvailableUtc`; doc_001 and doc_007 have none.
- `availability.json`: the availability editor's sample (screen 30), shaped like the planned `availability_rules` and `availability_exceptions` tables: UTC instants plus the rule zone, never local times. `schedules` has one row, `doc_002` in `America/Chicago`, 30-minute slots, 2 hours' notice: seven weekly rules (Sun 11:00 to 14:00; Mon, Tue and Wed 9:00 to 12:00; Tue also 13:00 to 15:00; Thu 8:00 to 11:00; Sat 9:00 to 11:00, Chicago time), each stored as its first occurrence in the week of 4 Oct 2026 and repeating at that wall-clock time, so the same rule moves by an hour in Riyadh after the US DST change on 1 Nov 2026. Two exceptions (a day off, extra hours on a Friday) and `addedException` (the day off the `?state=exceptionAdded` preview adds) were authored for 12 to 15 Nov and are shifted like the profile slots, in whole days on the Chicago clock, so they always fall in the coming week. Not generated from `profiles.json`, whose slots stay hand-authored until `lib/slots.ts` feeds the profile (task 2.6).
- `payouts.json`: the signed-in doctor's (doc_002) Stripe Connect Express account in four states for screen 35 (`/professional/payouts?state=`): `notStarted` (no account yet), `inProgress` (onboarding unfinished; Stripe still needs the bank account and terms), `enabled` (charges and payouts on, the next payout and the last two), `restricted` (switched off; Stripe needs a photo ID and a proof of address). Fields mirror what the `account.updated` webhook stores (task 3.1); requirement items are Stripe's own paths. Account ids are fake (`acct_sample_*`); payout dates go through `sampleInstant`.

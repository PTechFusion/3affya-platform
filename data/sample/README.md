# Sample data

Synthetic, for the prototype only. Never put a real patient document or a real NPI record here; real registry data arrives through `scripts/` in task 1.5 and real patient data never leaves production.

- `doctors.json`: 20 doctors, 8 verified, 4 pending, 8 unclaimed. Field names match the planned schema (task 1.4).
- `specialties.json`: 24 of the 104 specialty groups, bilingual, with sample counts. The full list comes from the content document in task 1.5.
- `bookings.json`: three bookings in the three money states the patient portal shows. Amounts in minor units; the three lines sum to the total.
- `body-systems.json`: the body systems that group the specialty directory's full list (screen 4), bilingual, each listing specialty slugs. Editorial draft for Dr Ayman; the 104-group mapping comes with task 1.5.
- `invites.json`: three claim invites for screen 9 (`/claim/[token]`): `demo-valid` for `doc_013` (unclaimed), `demo-expired` for `doc_014`, `demo-used` for `doc_010` (already claimed, so that profile is pending). Any other token is forged. Real invites store only a hash of the token and expire 14 days after sending (task 2.3); the valid demo expires far ahead so the prototype link keeps working. Emails use the reserved `example.com` domain.
- `profiles.json`: one row per doctor with what the profile page (screens 6 and 7) shows beyond the card: group practice and the registry source and date for unclaimed and pending rows; the named board, licence state and the next open times (UTC) for verified rows. `slotsUtc[0]` equals the doctor's `nextAvailableUtc`; doc_001 and doc_007 have none.
- `availability.json`: the availability editor's sample (screen 30), shaped like the planned `availability_rules` and `availability_exceptions` tables: UTC instants plus the rule zone, never local times. `schedules` has one row, `doc_002` in `America/Chicago`, 30-minute slots, 2 hours' notice: seven weekly rules (Sun 11:00 to 14:00; Mon, Tue and Wed 9:00 to 12:00; Tue also 13:00 to 15:00; Thu 8:00 to 11:00; Sat 9:00 to 11:00, Chicago time), each stored as its first occurrence in the week of 4 Oct 2026 and repeating at that wall-clock time, so the same rule moves by an hour in Riyadh after the US DST change on 1 Nov 2026. Two exceptions (a day off, extra hours on a Friday) and `addedException` (the day off the `?state=exceptionAdded` preview adds) were authored for 12 to 15 Nov and are shifted like the profile slots, in whole days on the Chicago clock, so they always fall in the coming week. Not generated from `profiles.json`, whose slots stay hand-authored until `lib/slots.ts` feeds the profile (task 2.6).
- `payouts.json`: the signed-in doctor's (doc_002) Stripe Connect Express account in four states for screen 35 (`/professional/payouts?state=`): `notStarted` (no account yet), `inProgress` (onboarding unfinished; Stripe still needs the bank account and terms), `enabled` (charges and payouts on, the next payout and the last two), `restricted` (switched off; Stripe needs a photo ID and a proof of address). Fields mirror what the `account.updated` webhook stores (task 3.1); requirement items are Stripe's own paths. Account ids are fake (`acct_sample_*`); payout dates go through `sampleInstant`.
- `memberships.json`: one professional's membership (`doc_003`) in the four states of screen 33 (`/professional/membership`): `none` (Claimed, free), `active` (Member, monthly, renewing at `currentPeriodEndUtc`), `past_due` (the monthly renewal failed; the `membershipGraceDays` grace period counts from `paymentFailedAtUtc`) and `cancelled` (yearly, cancelled in the Stripe portal and ending at `currentPeriodEndUtc`). No prices are stored here; they come from `config/business.ts`. `timeZone` is the doctor's own. Real rows are written only by the Stripe webhook handler (task 3.5).
- `introductions.json`: five inbox rows for `doc_002` (screen 32, `/professional/inbox`): `int_001` and `int_002` confirmed and upcoming (int_002 with shared summary `sh_001`, not yet reviewed), `int_003` a new introduction request with the concern and age group from the intake, `int_004` a visit yesterday not yet marked done (shared summary `sh_002`, reviewed), `int_005` done. Times are authored for 3 to 12 Nov 2026 and shifted with `sampleInstant`, so the upcoming visits stay ahead. Join links use the reserved `example.com` domain.
- `summaries.json`: three synthetic records summaries (`summaries`, one per record set `rs_001`..`rs_003`, bilingual) and the three shares of them with `doc_002` (`shares`), for screens 36 and 26. Every item carries its source pages; `flags` hold missing documents and conflicts, `unreadable` the pages nothing was taken from. Instants shift with `sampleInstant`; document dates do not. Expiry is derived from `shareDefaultDays`; status from `viewedAtUtc` and the summary's `reviewedBy`: `sh_001` new, `sh_002` viewed, `sh_003` reviewed by doc_002.
- `builder.json`: what two doctors entered in the profile builder (screen 29) beyond the registry row: licence state and number, board status, video link and the names and sizes of the uploaded credential files (names only; the prototype never stores a file). `doc_002` is verified; `doc_009` is pending, and its submitted and decided instants and reviewer note feed the builder's submitted and rejected previews. Instants are authored around 9 Nov and shifted by `sampleInstant`.
- `us-states.json`: the 50 states and DC, bilingual, for the builder's city and licence pickers. Arabic names are AI-drafted (`needs_review`).
- `patient-settings.json`: the signed-in patient's settings for screen 28 (`/patient/settings`): Noura's name in Arabic and English letters, a reserved `example.com` email, a sample Saudi mobile number, `Asia/Riyadh`, Hijri dates on, the three optional emails (summary ready and summary opened on, news off), `hasUploads`, and `deletionRequestedAtUtc`, the instant the `?state=deletionRequested` preview asked for deletion (shifted with `sampleInstant`; the deletion date adds `accountData.deletionGraceDays` from `config/business.ts`).
- `receipts.json`: the payment facts behind each booking in `bookings.json` for screen 23 (`/patient/receipts`), as the Stripe webhooks would store them: a Stripe-style receipt number, the charge currency, when it was paid, the wallet and card brand with the last 4 digits (`0341` and `5520`, fake), and any cancellation and refund. `bk_001` paid with Apple Pay (visit upcoming), `bk_002` paid by card (visit done), `bk_003` cancelled by the patient three days before the visit and refunded. No amounts are stored here: the three lines come from the booking row as charged, and the refund amount from the refund rule in `lib/refunds.ts` (`fullRefundCutoffHours`). Instants are authored relative to the booking times and shifted with `sampleInstant`.
- `bookings.json`, rows `bk_004` to `bk_009` (screen 22, `/patient/bookings`): the signed-in sample patient Noura's (`samplePatient`) six bookings, read through `bookingsForPatient`. `bk_004` with doc_002 is the visit behind inbox row `int_002` (same time, same join link) and her next visit; `bk_005` (doc_006) is later; `bk_006` (doc_003, phone) and `bk_007` (doc_007) are done; `bk_008` (doc_004) was cancelled by the doctor and refunded in full (its original time is still ahead); `bk_009` (doc_008) was cancelled by Noura less than `fullRefundCutoffHours` before the visit, so `cancelled` with no refund. These rows add `joinUrl`, `bookedAtUtc`, `cancelledAtUtc` and `cancelledBy`; the three fee lines follow `lib/fees.ts` at each doctor's fee. Times are authored around 9 Nov 2026 and shifted with `sampleInstant`.
- `bookings.json` row `bk_211` (screen 21): an upcoming phone visit with `doc_003`, authored for 20 Nov 2026 (after `bk_001`, so `bk_001` stays the next visit), so the confirmation page's phone-call variant is reachable. Fees follow `feeLines(22000)`.
- `booking-visits.json` (screen 21): what a booking row does not carry, keyed by `bookingId`: the visit length, the doctor's meeting link for an upcoming video visit (`example.com`; null for phone visits and for visits that are over), and `cancelledAtUtc` for `bk_003` (three days before its visit, so inside the full-refund window). Read through `patientBookingById` in `lib/sample.ts`, which shifts every instant with `sampleInstant`.
- `bookings.json`, screen 27 addition: `bk_272`, a second upcoming video visit for Noura, with `doc_004` (Dr Karim Saleh), the doctor she can still share her summary with. Her visit with `doc_002` is `bk_004` (screen 22), the patient side of introduction `int_002`.
- `records.json`: the records screens (24 `/patient/records`, 25 `/patient/records/[id]`). File names, sizes and per-page states only; never a file. `draft` is the upload flow's preview (four accepted files, 12 pages, 11.1 MB, plus three not accepted: a ZIP, a 72.4 MB PDF and a password-locked PDF; `checking` lists the two still being checked in `?state=validating`). `sets` are record sets shaped like the planned `record_sets`, `record_files` and `record_pages` tables: `rs_001`..`rs_003` are the sets behind `sum_001`..`sum_003` (same owners: Noura, Yousef, Faisal; same page counts), and Noura also has `rs_004` processing (page 2 unreadable, pages 5 and 6 being read, 4 of 12 done), `rs_005` failed (page 4 of `lab-results-2026.pdf` damaged after three tries) and `rs_006` queued (the draft's four files, paid). Pages are numbered across the set in file order. Prices are not stored: the quote is derived from the file sizes with `quoteFor` and `config/business.ts`. Instants are authored around 7 to 9 Nov and shifted with `sampleInstant`.
- `audit.json` (screen 47, `/admin/audit`): the admin audit log, shaped like the planned append-only `audit_log` table. `entries` are 48 rows `aud_001`..`aud_048`, oldest first, each with the UTC instant, the actor id, the action (vocabulary in `lib/audit.ts`), the target (type, id and the related professional), the before and after of each changed field, extra details, a request id and, for Stripe-written rows, a fake `evt_sample_*` event id. Verification rows carry the primary-source checks (source, result, when, who checked) and the reviewer (rule A1); rejections and removals carry the note in both languages. `actors` names admins and patients by first name only (Sara is `sampleAdmin`; Khalid is a second admin; Noura, Yousef and Faisal are the patients of `rs_001`..`rs_003`); professionals resolve through `doctors.json`, and `doc_021` is a removed profile, so only its id remains. The rows reference real sample entities: the `doc_006` approval and `doc_009` rejection (with `builder.json`'s reason), invites `inv_002` and `inv_003`, bookings `bk_004`..`bk_009` and their Stripe payments and refund, payouts `po_sample_001`/`002`, record sets and shares `sh_001`..`sh_003`. Instants are authored for 12 Oct to 8 Nov 2026 and shifted with `sampleInstant`; read through `auditEntries` (newest first).
- `invites.json`, screen 46 addition (`/admin/outreach`): rows `inv_004` to `inv_013` add the tracking facts the admin list reads through `outreachInvites` in `lib/sample.ts`: `openedAtUtc` (the claim page was opened from the link; email opens are not tracked), `bouncedAtUtc` with `bounce` (`noMailbox` or `rejected`, as the Resend webhook reports it), `revokedAtUtc` and the email `language`. Statuses: `inv_004` opened (doc_015), `inv_005` bounced (doc_016), `inv_006` sent, not opened (doc_017), `inv_007` to `inv_010` claimed (doc_009, doc_011, doc_012 pending; doc_003 verified), `inv_011` opened then expired (doc_019), `inv_012` revoked (doc_018). `inv_013` (doc_020, `preview: "justSent"`) appears only in `?state=just-sent`, dated now. The first three rows are unchanged (screen 9 reads them unshifted); on screen 46 every instant goes through `sampleInstant` (authored up to 8 Nov 2026), so `inv_001` reads as an open invite with its far demo expiry. Tokens are synthetic (`smp46-*`); production stores only a hash.
- `removals.json`: six "remove me" requests for the admin queue (screen 41, `/admin/removals`), as screen 10's remove branch would store them: the name, NPI, email, reason and details the doctor typed, the page language, when the request arrived and when the confirmation link was opened, and any admin question or decision. All are for unclaimed registry profiles (`doc_015`..`doc_020`; `doc_013` and `doc_014` stay free for the claim demos). `rm_001` (doc_016) open, about 6 hours old, every check passed; `rm_002` (doc_018, sent in Arabic) open and amber, about 40 hours old; `rm_003` (doc_019) overdue, about 53 hours old; `rm_004` (doc_020) waiting on the doctor: name spelled differently, link not opened, an admin question paused the timer; `rm_005` (doc_017) confirmed, the listing removed (its profile previews as 410 with `?state=removed`); `rm_006` (doc_015) refused, asked for by someone other than the doctor. Unlike the other files, instants are shifted with `removalInstant`, which maps `authoredNowUtc` to the real now to the minute, so each request keeps its age in hours (the SLA states) whenever the prototype is opened. Emails use `example.com`.
